How ScholarTrack Works

A straightforward, four-step approach to keep your funding search organized from discovery to submission.

1

Build Profile

Add your course, year, state, and academic percentage to establish your base profile.

2

Explore & Filter

Filter through open scholarships based on criteria, deadline proximity, and study level.

3

Verify Criteria

Review required documents, income ceilings, and verify official announcement URLs.

4

Track Submission

Log submission dates, save your reference IDs, and monitor your deadlines in your dashboard.

Scholarship Source & Data Transparency

ScholarTrack is an informational tracking tool. All scholarship data originates from public portals of government departments, educational institutions, and accredited trusts. We do not issue scholarships, charge application commissions, or claim official affiliation. Compatibility matching scores are illustrative calculations designed to assist your discovery and do not represent formal eligibility decisions. Always verify final instructions on the official provider website.
